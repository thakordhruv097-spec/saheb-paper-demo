# Privacy Policy

**Saheb Paper Pvt. Ltd. — Stock, Production & Dispatch Management System**

*Last updated: September 6, 2026*

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Saheb Paper Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "the
Company") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data through its
internal Stock, Production & Dispatch Management System ("the System").

This System is an internal business tool used by Company employees,
operators, and authorized personnel to manage factory operations. It is
not a public-facing consumer application.

We are committed to handling personal data responsibly and in accordance
with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and its
associated Rules.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

**Employee / User Data**
- Full name
- Username / login ID
- Phone number (where provided)
- Email address (where provided)
- Role/designation within the Company
- Login activity and system usage logs

**Customer / Party Data**
- Business/party name
- Contact person name
- Phone number
- GSTIN (where applicable)
- Business address

**Vendor / Supplier Data**
- Business name
- Contact details
- GSTIN (where applicable)

**System Activity Data**
- Records of actions taken within the System (e.g. stock entries,
  dispatch confirmations, user management changes) — retained as an
  audit log for accountability and traceability purposes.

We do **not** collect any data beyond what is necessary to operate the
System for its intended business purpose.

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

Personal data is collected and processed solely for the following
purposes:

- To authenticate and authorize System users (login, role-based access)
- To operate core business functions: raw material tracking, production
  logging, dispatch management, and reporting
- To maintain traceability records for finished goods and deliveries
- To maintain an audit trail of who performed which action, for
  accountability and dispute resolution
- To communicate with customers and vendors in the ordinary course of
  business

We do not use this data for any purpose unrelated to operating the
System, and we do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third
party.

---

## 4. How We Store and Protect Data

- Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing (bcrypt)
  and are never stored or visible in plain text.
- Access to the System is controlled through Role-Based Access Control
  (RBAC) — each user can only view and act on data relevant to their
  role.
- All administrative and data-modifying actions are recorded in an
  immutable audit log.
- Data is hosted on secure infrastructure, with access restricted to
  authorized Company personnel and the System's technical administrators.
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect
  personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or
  destruction.

---

## 5. Who Can Access Your Data

Access to personal data within the System is limited strictly to:

- The individual whose data it is (where applicable, e.g. an employee
  viewing their own profile)
- Authorized personnel whose role requires access to that data (e.g. an
  Admin or Plant Manager)
- Technical administrators, solely for the purpose of maintaining and
  supporting the System

We do not share personal data with any external party except where
required by law.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the
purposes outlined in this policy, or as required to comply with
applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations. Data relating
to a former employee, customer, or vendor may be retained for a
reasonable period thereafter for record-keeping and legal purposes.

---

## 7. Your Rights

In accordance with the DPDP Act, individuals whose personal data we
process have the right to:

- Request access to the personal data we hold about them
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Request erasure of their personal data, subject to our legal and
  operational retention requirements
- Withdraw consent for processing, where processing is based on consent
- Raise a grievance regarding how their data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details
below.

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect
individuals whose data we hold, we will take prompt steps to assess and
contain the breach and will notify affected individuals and relevant
authorities as required under applicable law.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes
in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top
of this document will reflect the most recent revision.
