1. Executive Project Overview & Scope Delivered

This custom-engineered Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system has been designed specifically for paper mill manufacturing operations, providing complete digitisation from Raw Material Intake to Rewinder QR Labeling and Dispatch Gate Pass Generation.

DELIVERED SYSTEM SCOPE & PLATFORMS:
• 📱 Android Native Mobile App (.apk) — Operating on phones & tablets for plant operators.
• 💻 Windows Portable Desktop App (.exe) — Installed on weighbridge & office PCs.
• 🌐 Web Browser Cloud Access — Instant access on laptops & remote management PCs.
• 🏷️ QR Engine & Label Generator — Instant stickers for paper reels (TSC Thermal Printers / A4).
• 📷 Dual Camera & Laser Barcode Gun Scanner — Live smartphone scan + handheld scanner support.
• 🔒 9 Role-Based Access Accounts — Admin, Boiler, ETP, Machine, Rewinder, Dispatch, Store.
